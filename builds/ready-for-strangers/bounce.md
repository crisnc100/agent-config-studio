I would not approve this plan as written. The largest gap is that **rejecting a CLI’s stream is not deterministic containment of that CLI**.

1. **[P1] Init enforcement cannot guarantee “before any tool call.”**  
   [plan.md:32](/Users/cortega/Documents/Projects/personal/agent-config-studio-wt/agent-config-studio-configurable-roots/builds/ready-for-strangers/plan.md:32) assumes ACS controls tool execution. The child can execute before ACS handles stdout—even if it emits init first. Killing the child also does not establish that spawned MCP processes are dead. Therefore layer (a) alone cannot substantiate the promised containment. Require prevention before execution, or disable affected Assist invocations when that cannot be established. A fake CLI should attempt an observable side effect before init and immediately after emitting invalid init; “no edit proposed” does not test this property.
