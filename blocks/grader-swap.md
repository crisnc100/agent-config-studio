**Cross-vendor preferred, never a blocker.** The reviewer or grader should not share the builder's vendor; prefer one from the other vendor when it is available. Never wait on a grader: if the preferred one is out of quota or unavailable, use the other right away. Say which one ran and, when it was same-vendor, why.
